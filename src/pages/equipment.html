Method

  • Downtime: hours from a breakdown being reported to being resolved; open breakdowns count up to now.
  • MTTR: mean downtime of resolved breakdowns.
  • MTBF: (fleet operating hours in the window − downtime) ÷ number of breakdowns.
  • Availability: 1 − downtime ÷ fleet hours, for equipment not decommissioned.
  • PM due: last PM (or installation) + interval; “due soon” within 14 days. Intervals should follow the manufacturer and local policy (see WHO, Medical equipment maintenance programme overview, WHO Medical device technical series, 2011).
