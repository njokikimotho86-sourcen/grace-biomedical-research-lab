About Grace

Medical Engineering graduate and practising biomedical engineer in Nairobi.

Biography

Grace Njoki Kimotho is a Kenyan Medical Engineering graduate of Kirinyaga University and a biomedical engineer at Paula Solutions Limited in Nairobi. Her work joins two worlds: the bench, where she installs, commissions, maintains and repairs medical equipment, and the computer, where she builds finite element models of orthopaedic implants and explores how smart glasses and AI can bring expertise to facilities that lack it.

Her research experience spans finite element analysis of the hip implant head–stem interface and mixed-reality smart glasses for remote clinical support. She was selected by her university to represent it internationally in Rwanda, where she built the QR-based digital identity system for a gemstone trade platform.

Career objective

To pursue postgraduate study and research at the intersection of medical engineering, computational modelling, artificial intelligence and healthcare technology, and to build practical, affordable healthcare solutions for Africa.
