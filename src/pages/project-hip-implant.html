Hip implant research

Reproduction study of a published finite element analysis of wear and fatigue at the modular head–stem taper of a total hip implant. Geometry rebuilt in CATIA V5; nine load and material cases solved in ANSYS Workbench 2024 R2 with frictional contact; wear by modified Archard; fatigue by stress–life with Goodman correction.
