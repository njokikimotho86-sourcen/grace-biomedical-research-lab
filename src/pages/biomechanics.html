Equations used

  • Neck: A = πd²/4, σaxial = F cosθ / A, σbending = 32 F sinθ L / (πd³), σmax = σaxial + σbending; strain ε = σ/E.
  • Equivalent stress (conservative): √(σmax² + 3τ²) with τ = 4V/(3A).
  • Goodman: σa/Se + σm/Su = 1/n (ductile metals only).
  • Hertz, ball in cup: R′ = R₁R₂/(R₂ − R₁), 1/E* = (1−ν₁²)/E₁ + (1−ν₂²)/E₂, a = (3FR′/4E*)^⅓, p₀ = 3F/(2πa²). Invalid when a is large relative to R₁ (conformal contact).
  • Archard, bearing: V = k F s. Taper (modified): h = k p δ N, V = h × engaged area.
