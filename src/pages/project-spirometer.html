3D-printed spirometer replacement component

When a client's spirometer needed a spare part, Grace designed the part as a 3D model and 3D-printed a replacement so the device could return to service. The case shows how local 3D printing can reduce equipment downtime in Kenyan facilities.

The work followed a design-to-print workflow: measure the original part and its mating features, model it in 3D CAD, print, check the fit on the device, then confirm the spirometer worked correctly before handing it back. The part is a maintenance replacement, not a certified medical device component, and is documented with the lab's twelve-section template.
